2026 U.S. Occupational Licensing by State

Share of the state workforce that must hold an occupational license and typical licensing fee ranges, by state. National baseline: roughly 1 in 4 U.S. workers (about 25%) is licensed; the share varies widely by state (Nevada ~31% highest, South Carolina ~12% lowest, per the 2015 White House report). Fee ranges are estimated from Institute for Justice state average licensure fees (2022).
